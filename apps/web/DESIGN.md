# INTEGRA visual system

This is the visual source of truth for `apps/web`. The direction is **editorial precision**, adapted from the Genesis system (designmd.ai/chef/genesis): quietly confident, bold display typography, generous spacing, and gallery-frame surfaces. It should feel professional and modern without being sterile, with high information density balanced by breathing room.

INTEGRA keeps Genesis's gallery-frame idea but leads with borders: every surface has a 1px `hairline` border, and shadows are **small and low-opacity** (at most 8% black), only hinting at elevation.

## Typography

- **Display:** General Sans (Fontshare stylesheet in `app/layout.tsx`), weight 600, tracking `-0.03em` (`font-display tracking-display`). `h1`–`h3` use it by default.
- **Body and UI:** DM Sans (`next/font`), weights 400 and 500 only.
- **Code and IDs:** JetBrains Mono (`font-mono`) for claim numbers, ICD-10 and INA-CBG codes, facility codes.

Never swap the display and body faces, and use no more than two body weights on a screen.

| Role            | Size | Tailwind        | Use                                                     |

## Spacing and layout

- 4px base grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96px.
- Component padding: small 8×12, medium 10×16, large 12×24.
- Workspace content: `max-w-7xl` (1280px), 32px side padding from `md`, 16px on phones; 32px top padding on phones and 40px from `md`.
- Breakpoints: phones are below `md` (768px), tablets `md` to `lg`, desktops from `lg` (1024px). The sidebar is fixed only from `lg`, so tablets keep the full width. Layouts that need more room switch at `xl` (1280px): the Kartu Klaim side column, four stat tiles in a row, and the three-column Dashboard chart row. Verify every page at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px wide with no page-level horizontal scroll.
- Use `min-h-dvh` for full-height screens so mobile browser toolbars do not hide content.
- Grid and stack gaps between cards: 16–24px.

## Radius

| Radius | Use                                             |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 4px    | Inline code, `mark` highlights                  |
| 6px    | Buttons, inputs, selects, nav links, icon tiles |
| 8px    | Inset boxes, dropdowns, menus, list frames      |
| 12px   | Cards, panels, tables, dialogs, empty states    |
| 9999px | Chips and badges, avatars, status dots          |

Keep cards at 12px and controls at 6px; do not mix them.

## Elevation: border first, small shadows

The shadow scale in `globals.css` is capped: `shadow-xs` (1px, 4%) for resting surfaces, `shadow-sm` for chart tooltips, `shadow-md` for menus and popovers, and `shadow-lg` and above share one 8px, 8% value for sheets, dialogs, and the tour card. Do not add arbitrary `shadow-[...]` values.

- Cards, panels, stat tiles, tables, buttons (primary and outline), and inputs rest on a 1px `hairline` border with `shadow-xs`.
- Interactive rows and cards change background (`hover:bg-subtle`) instead of lifting further.
- Overlays dim with a solid `bg-ink/55` layer (the tour spotlight uses four such panels around its target and a 2px `primary` border). The tour adds a faint `backdrop-blur-[2px]` behind its card so the page reads as muted, never unreadable; around a spotlight the blur is one full-viewport layer clipped around the target, because blurring each panel separately leaves visible seams.
- Focus: inputs turn their border `primary` (INTEGRA green) with a 3px `primary/15` ring; other controls use the global 2px `primary` outline.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary is INTEGRA green fill, white text, 6px radius, medium weight, hover `primary-hover`. Outline (secondary) is `surface` with a green `primary` border and green text, hover `primary-wash`. Ghost has no border or fill; on hover it gets a subtle background and green text. Destructive stays red text with a red border, because red marks risky actions. All shift up 1px on hover when motion is allowed. Sizes: sm 32px, default 38px, lg 44px. Use one primary button per view section.
- **Where green appears:** primary, outline, and ghost-hover buttons; text links; active filter chips; checked checkboxes, radios, and switches (the Kartu Klaim decision options are the exception: the chosen option takes its action's tone, `success` for Setujui, `warning` for Minta klarifikasi, and `error` for Eskalasi, as a solid border over a 10% tint, and its radio border and dot take the same tone); pagination and active tabs; progress bars and spinners (the AI loading bar, tour steps); focus rings; the 2px tour spotlight border; and selected table rows (`primary-wash`). Status chips, chart colors, destructive buttons, and the logo are excluded.
- **Cards and panels:** `rounded-xl border border-hairline bg-surface`, 16px padding for compact side panels and 24px for content panels. Panel headers are separated by a `hairline` bottom border.
- **Inset boxes:** `rounded-lg border border-hairline bg-canvas` for supporting explanations, evidence that was not found, and money breakdowns.
- **Inputs:** 1px `hairline` border, `surface` background, 6px radius, 10×14px padding, 14px text, `ink-muted` placeholder. Error state turns the border red.
- **Chips (`Pill` in `components/claim-pills.tsx`):** pill shape, 12px text, medium weight. Tones: `outline`, `neutral` (gray), `success`, `warning`, `error`, `outpatient` (blue), `inpatient` (violet), and `inverse` (ink, for the INTEGRA AI mark). Filter chips are the only green chips: `surface` with a `hairline` border, and green fill with white text when active. Status chips and badges keep their semantic tones and never turn green.
- **Tables and lists:** stacked rows with 1px dividers and 1px `hairline` column dividers (none after the last column), 12px×16px cell padding, headers centered, cells left-aligned except the No and date columns (Keterangan rawat, LOS, Terakhir dibaca, and the claim queue's Aksi column, which shows "Dianalisis AI" with the analysis date once a claim is analyzed and the Analisis button before), which are centered with `CENTERED_COLUMN_CLASS_NAME` from `components/data-table/centered-column.ts`, with claim numbers kept on one line, checkboxes with an `ink-muted` border so they read as selectable, `canvas` header row with uppercase overline labels, `hover:bg-subtle` on rows. Tables live inside a 12px bordered frame and may scroll horizontally inside that frame on narrow desktops; let long text cells wrap (`whitespace-normal` with a min width) before relying on scroll. The nine-column claim queue uses 8px side padding so it fits from 1440px and scrolls inside its frame below that. Below `lg`, each table renders as a card list instead (`divide-y` rows with 16px padding, the same pills and actions, and a `TableSortSelect` "Urutkan" select in the toolbar replacing the sortable headers); any `data-tour` target on a row is repeated on its card. Every data table uses `components/data-table`: a toolbar row above the header (`hairline` bottom border) holding a 36px search input group and 36px filter comboboxes labelled "Label: value" with the value left-aligned right after the label, opening a popover whose search input filters the options as the user types (`TableFilterSelect`, shadcn Popover + Command), stacked full width on phones; sortable column headers that cycle ascending and descending with an arrow icon and `aria-sort`; a "no matching rows" message with a reset button; and pagination at 10 rows with outline Sebelumnya/Berikutnya buttons in a bordered footer. Tables backed by the API (Antrean Klaim, Eskalasi, Ringkasan Faskes) page on the server with `useServerTableControls`: the API filters, searches (debounced 300ms), sorts, and returns one page, and the previous page stays visible at 60% opacity while the next loads. Only the in-memory import preview uses `useTableControls` on the client.
- **Care type:** a `CareTypePill` marks every claim: blue `outpatient` with `LuStethoscope` for Rawat jalan, violet `inpatient` with `LuBedDouble` for Rawat inap, both as a 10% tint with a 30% border and their `*-ink` text. These two hues mark care type only, so they never collide with the decision colors. The queue's Keterangan rawat column shows the chip above Masuk and Pulang dates (Kunjungan for rawat jalan), the LOS column shows `formatLengthOfStay` ("4 hari", or "-" for rawat jalan), and the Kartu Klaim places the chip right after the claim number and gives a Keterangan rawat summary cell with the chip above labelled rows (Tanggal masuk, Tanggal pulang, LOS for Rawat Inap; Tanggal kunjungan and LOS "-" for Rawat Jalan). Chip labels are title case: Rawat Jalan, Rawat Inap.
- **Verification card:** under the claim summary, the Kartu Klaim shows Hasil verifikasi in two halves. The left half has the SEP number and the latest decision (its decision chip, reason, verifier, and time, plus a Dokumen yang perlu dilengkapi di VEDIKA checklist for Minta klarifikasi, `warning` dashed circle while missing and `success` check once received). The right half is a `canvas` box with Rekomendasi INTEGRA, an outline Asumsi chip, the recommended decision chip, the score in display type, the strongest finding, and one caption stating the assumed rule; before analysis it says the score appears after the AI analysis.
- **Decision chips:** `DecisionActionPill` reads Setuju (`success`), Minta Klarifikasi (`warning`), and Eskalasi (`error`) from `DECISION_RESULT_LABELS`, while the decision panel options keep the verb labels Setujui, Minta klarifikasi, and Eskalasi.
- **Decision panel:** the option matching the recommendation carries an outline Rekomendasi INTEGRA chip, and choosing Minta klarifikasi opens a `warning`-tinted Dokumen yang kurang checkbox list, prefilled from Uji Ada missing evidence and marked "(disarankan dari Uji Ada)", which needs at least one checked document.
- **Diagnoses card:** the Kartu Klaim Diagnosis card lists ICD-10 diagnoses, then a `Tindakan (ICD-9-CM)` caption with the claim's procedure codes in the same bordered list style, or "Tidak ada kode tindakan." when there are none.
- **Similar pattern:** a warning `SimilarPatternPill` ("Pola mirip", `LuRepeat`) sits with the priority and severity chips in the queue, and the Kartu Klaim aside shows a Pola mirip panel under the decision panel: one `text-small` sentence with the facility's secondary diagnosis share in semibold, an `ink-secondary` caption with the 80th percentile threshold, the peer median, and that the marker is for tracing rather than proof, then the facility's other claims with the same diagnosis and procedure codes, each stacked as its SEP number link (allowed to break), admission date, and status chip so long SEP numbers never collide with the chip.
- **Queue status chips:** pill toggles above the claim queue, one per status plus Semua, each with its claim count from `GET /claims/statistics` in a small inner pill (`subtle`, or white at 20% on the active green chip), and one `ink-secondary` line under the chips describing which claims the active view holds.
- **Navigation:** the shadcn Sidebar (`components/app-sidebar.tsx`) on a vertical gradient from `--sidebar` (#132e2d) to `--sidebar-bottom` (#0c5c55), drawn on `[data-sidebar='sidebar']` in `globals.css`, with white text and 12% white `sidebar-border` and `sidebar-accent`: the white logo (`integra-logo-white.png`) left-aligned on top, role-based groups with overline labels (verifiers see Verifikasi, supervisors only Supervisor; the logo links to each role's home), and a footer with the signed-in user card, Panduan (verifiers only), Ciutkan menu, and Keluar. Menu buttons are 36px, 14px medium; every sidebar text is solid white, never dimmed; hover and the active item use `sidebar-accent`, and the active item adds a 2px white bar on its left edge. From `lg` the sidebar collapses to a 48px icon rail that shows the white `integra-mark-white.png` instead of the logo (footer button, the edge rail, or Ctrl/⌘+B); collapsed items show their label in a tooltip, and the state persists in the `sidebar_state` cookie. Width and slide transitions last 300ms `ease-out` and stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Below `lg` the sidebar becomes a vaul drawer (`components/ui/drawer.tsx`) opened from a solid sticky 56px header (no backdrop blur, which stutters during the slide on phones). vaul slides it with a compositor-only transform on the `ease-drawer` curve (fast start, long gentle stop) over an `ink/55` overlay, it can be swiped closed, and it closes once a page is chosen; `globals.css` cuts its motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Dashboards (`components/dashboard-panels.tsx`):** charts live only on the supervisor Dashboard (`/overview`, `components/overview`) and Penggunaan AI (`/ai-usage`, `components/ai-usage`) pages; work pages such as Antrean Klaim and Ringkasan Faskes show tables only. `StatTile` (overline label, subhead value, caption hint) inside `StatTileGrid`: one column on phones, two from `sm`, and four from `xl`, so rupiah figures never overflow their tile; `ChartPanel` cards (24px padding, body-weight heading, small description) holding shadcn charts. Gridlines are `hairline`, axes have no lines, bars have a 4px end radius, and every chart has a text legend (`ChartLegendList`) with values so color is never the only cue. INTEGRA green is not used in charts; their semantic colors stay unchanged.
- **Icons:** Lucide outline icons imported from `react-icons/lu` (`LuSearch`, `LuArrowUpDown`, and so on); type icon props as `IconType` from `react-icons`. Do not add `lucide-react` or another icon set. Icons are 16px in buttons and menus, 14px in chips and sortable headers, and inherit text color. When adding a shadcn component, replace its `lucide-react` imports with the `Lu*` equivalent.
- **Quotes and highlights:** document quotes have a 2px `ink-muted` left rule; identical text across claims is a `warning/20` `mark`.
- **Dialogs:** shadcn AlertDialog on `surface` with a 12px radius, `hairline` border, and `shadow-lg`; a 40px `subtle` icon tile, a body-weight General Sans title, and `ink-secondary` description. Ask for confirmation (`components/confirm-dialog.tsx`, "Tidak" outline plus a "Ya, …" primary button) before paid AI analysis and before Keluar; state the consequence, including how many unread documents the AI will read. Errors and outcomes the user must read before continuing, such as a failed login or sign-up, use the one-button `components/notice-dialog.tsx`. ## Decoration and motion

The only decorative element is the static dot grid (`lib/dot-grid.ts`, `hairline` dots on a 20px grid) on the login brand panel and behind the status pages, where it fades out toward the edges with a radial mask. Apart from the sidebar gradient, there are no gradients, illustrations, or glass effects. Transitions last 200ms and run only under `motion-safe`; the desktop sidebar's width uses 300ms `ease-out` and the mobile sheet uses `ease-drawer`, so each reads as one smooth motion.

## Accessibility

Use semantic HTML, explicit labels, visible focus, and keyboard-operable controls. Body and metadata text use `ink` or `ink-secondary` (never `ink-muted`). Do not rely on color alone: chips pair color with an icon or label. Do not use pure black (`#000`) for text.

## Do and don't

**Do:** use `canvas` for the page and `surface` for cards, separate everything with `hairline` borders, use INTEGRA green only for what can be clicked or is selected, keep General Sans for headings and DM Sans for everything else, and keep the 4px grid.

**Don't:** add shadows larger than the capped scale or without a border, use INTEGRA green for static text, money, or status chips, put more than one primary button in a section, use `ink-muted` for readable text, mix 12px and 6px radii on the same kind of element, or add decorative gradients and illustrations.
